Technique

Your First Pull-Up

Pull-ups develop upper-body pulling capacity, but they require adequate grip, shoulder control and a stable bar.

Start with support

Practice comfortable active hangs and shoulder-blade control on a secure bar. Use an assisted pull-up machine or band if appropriate.

Develop the pattern

Perform assisted repetitions through a controlled range, avoiding swinging or jerking. Row variations can complement the practice.

Progress patiently

Reduce assistance gradually. The time needed to achieve an unassisted repetition varies considerably between people.

Develop the prerequisites

Secure rows, supported hangs and scapular control exercises can help build pulling capacity before full repetitions.

Use assistance deliberately

Foot support and correctly attached resistance bands can reduce the load. Controlled lowering can be used when you can safely reach the top position.

Avoid momentum

Practice smooth pulling and lowering rather than using a large kick to complete a repetition. Regress when form becomes unpredictable.

Educational guidance only. Adapt exercise to your abilities and seek professional advice when needed.
