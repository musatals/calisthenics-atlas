Calisthenics at Home

Build a practical routine in a small space.

Start with what you have

A floor and a secure chair can support squats, bridges, lunges, planks and some incline push-ups. Equipment is optional for many foundational movements.

Inspect supports

Household furniture may slide or tip. Only use stable surfaces suitable for the load. Pull-up bars and rings require proper anchoring.

A short session

Choose a squat, a pushing movement, a pulling movement if you have a safe setup, and a trunk exercise. Perform controlled sets with rest as needed.

Make consistency easier

Keep the routine short enough to repeat. Record progress and increase difficulty only when movements are reliable.

General educational guidance only. Exercise within comfortable limits, and seek individualized professional advice when appropriate.
