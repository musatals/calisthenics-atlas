Getting Started with Calisthenics

Calisthenics uses your own body mass as resistance. You can begin with simple movements and scale them as strength and coordination improve.

Choose the basics

Build sessions around a pushing movement, a pulling movement when equipment permits, a squat or lunge pattern, and trunk stability. Walking or other aerobic activity complements strength training.

Build a manageable week

Start with two or three nonconsecutive full-body sessions if your recovery allows. Use a difficulty that lets you perform repetitions with control.

Track meaningful progress

Record exercises, sets, repetitions and perceived difficulty. Improve one variable at a time, and keep the routine sustainable.

Educational guidance only. Adapt exercise to your abilities and seek professional advice when needed.
