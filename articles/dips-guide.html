Dips: Preparation and Alternatives

Build pressing support without rushing deep dips.

Why dips can be demanding

Dips create substantial shoulder and elbow demands. The full depth is not a requirement for everyone.

Prepare support strength

Use push-ups and controlled support holds on stable parallel bars. Foot-supported and assisted versions help regulate effort.

Perform comfortable repetitions

Keep the movement controlled; lower only through a comfortable range and press back to a stable support position.

Alternatives are valid

Push-up and pike push-up progressions can develop pressing strength without needing deep dips. Choose what suits your joints and goals.

General educational guidance only. Exercise within comfortable limits, and seek individualized professional advice when appropriate.
