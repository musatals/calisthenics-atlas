The Push-Up: Technique and Progressions

The push-up is a scalable upper-body pushing exercise involving the chest, shoulders, arms and trunk.

Set up

Place your hands at a comfortable width, brace your trunk and maintain a roughly straight line from shoulders to heels or knees.

Move with control

Lower within a comfortable range, then press away from the floor without letting your lower back sag. Breathing should remain natural.

Choose your level

Try wall or incline push-ups first. Progress toward floor push-ups as you can control the full movement; increase challenge only when ready.

Educational guidance only. Adapt exercise to your abilities and seek professional advice when needed.
