Programming

Build a Balanced Weekly Plan

Organize pushing, pulling, leg and core training.

Choose your frequency

A manageable starting point for many adults is two nonconsecutive full-body sessions. A third session can be added if recovery and schedule allow.

Cover the major patterns

Include a squat or lunge, a push, a pull and a trunk exercise. Pick easier variations that allow consistent, repeatable technique.

Plan a sample session

Example: chair squats 2 × 8, incline push-ups 2 × 8, supported rows 2 × 8, glute bridges 2 × 10 and dead bugs 2 × 6 per side. These are examples, not mandatory targets.

Adjust progressively

Write down what you did and how hard it felt. Add repetitions before adding a set or choosing a harder variation, as appropriate.

Keep aerobic activity

Walking, cycling or other aerobic activity complements strength and skills rather than competing with them.

General educational guidance only. Exercise within comfortable limits, and seek individualized professional advice when appropriate.
