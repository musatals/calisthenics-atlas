Bodyweight Rows: A Pulling Foundation

Develop horizontal pulling strength safely.

Why rows matter

Rows train horizontal pulling and can balance a program that includes push-ups. The difficulty depends largely on body angle and where your feet are placed.

Safe setup

Use only a bar or rings rated for exercise and attached securely. Do not hang from unstable tables or improvised anchors.

Technique

Start with a straight, braced body. Pull the chest toward the handles or bar while the shoulders and elbows move comfortably. Lower smoothly.

Progressions

Stand more upright to make the movement easier. Walk feet farther forward to become more horizontal only when your setup is secure and repetitions remain controlled.

General educational guidance only. Exercise within comfortable limits, and seek individualized professional advice when appropriate.
