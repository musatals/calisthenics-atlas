How to Handle Training Plateaus

Identify obstacles and make useful adjustments.

Verify the plateau

Look for a pattern across multiple sessions rather than judging one difficult workout.

Check the obvious variables

Sleep, food intake, stress, inconsistent sessions and excessive fatigue can affect performance. Check whether exercise difficulty jumped too quickly.

Modify one variable

Try improving range consistency, using a slightly easier variation, adding a rest day, or changing the rep target before changing the entire plan.

Revisit the goal

Progress can also appear as better control, steadier breathing or shorter recovery needs, not only a higher repetition total.

General educational guidance only. Exercise within comfortable limits, and seek individualized professional advice when appropriate.
