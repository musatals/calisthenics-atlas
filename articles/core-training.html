Core Training Beyond Crunches

Learn trunk control, bracing and scalable holds.

What core training involves

Trunk muscles help transfer force and control unwanted movement. Effective training is not limited to spinal flexion or crunches.

Start with coordination

Dead bugs and bird dogs offer low-equipment ways to rehearse bracing while moving your arms and legs.

Build holding capacity

Progress to forearm planks, side planks and hollow tuck holds. Use shorter levers or shorter holds if your back starts arching or you cannot breathe normally.

Beyond longer holds

Once a hold is easy to control, add a more demanding variation instead of extending the duration indefinitely. Quality matters more than an arbitrary time target.

General educational guidance only. Exercise within comfortable limits, and seek individualized professional advice when appropriate.
