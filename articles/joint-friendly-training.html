Safety

Training with Joint Comfort in Mind

Choose ranges and progressions that suit you.

Individual tolerance matters

There is no single universally comfortable joint position. Use a range and variation that lets you move with control.

Manage load

If a movement causes discomfort, reduce the demand through leverage, support, volume or range. Avoid repeatedly pushing through sharp pain.

Warm up for the task

Gradually introduce the movement and build toward the working variation with lighter repetitions.

Know when to seek help

Persistent or worsening pain, weakness, numbness or other concerning symptoms deserve assessment by an appropriate healthcare professional. This site cannot diagnose injuries.

General educational guidance only. Exercise within comfortable limits, and seek individualized professional advice when appropriate.
