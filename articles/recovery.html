Recovery, Rest and Consistency

Recovery supports adaptation and helps keep a training routine sustainable.

Distribute effort

Alternate demanding sessions with easier activity or rest as needed. Muscles and connective tissues may adapt at different rates.

Support recovery habits

Prioritize adequate sleep, hydration and nutrition. The best plan is one that fits your life and can be repeated consistently.

Respond to warning signs

Sharp pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms warrant stopping the activity. Persistent symptoms should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional.

Educational guidance only. Adapt exercise to your abilities and seek professional advice when needed.
