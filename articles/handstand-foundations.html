Handstand Foundations

Prepare your wrists, shoulders and balance.

Readiness first

Handstands impose considerable weight on wrists and shoulders. Begin with regular comfortable weight-bearing and active shoulder positioning.

Build gradually

Practice planks, pike holds and supported inversions with appropriate space, reliable surfaces and an exit strategy.

Use the wall intelligently

A wall hold can teach orientation and alignment, but only practice it if you can enter and exit safely. A qualified coach is valuable when learning inversions.

Common pitfalls

Do not kick blindly toward furniture, force wrist extension or chase fatigue. Stop when control declines.

General educational guidance only. Exercise within comfortable limits, and seek individualized professional advice when appropriate.
