Our mission

Make movement knowledge accessible.

Calisthenics Atlas is an educational portal for learning bodyweight training through structured explanations, progressive practice and clear safety guidance.

Editorial principles

We distinguish practical coaching suggestions from scientific claims, avoid guaranteed outcomes, and aim to make training approachable at different experience levels.

Roadmap

Upcoming expansions may include illustrated exercise profiles, interactive skill trees, reference-backed research summaries, multilingual content, and accessible training plans.
