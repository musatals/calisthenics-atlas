Plan your week

Three adaptable plans to teach how to assemble a balanced, sustainable bodyweight routine. These are examples rather than individualized prescriptions.

Beginner • 2 days a week

Foundation

Repeat two full-body sessions with rest days between them.

  1. Chair squat — 1–2 × 6–10
  2. Incline push-up — 1–2 × 6–10
  3. Secure supported row — 1–2 × 6–10
  4. Glute bridge — 1–2 × 8–12
  5. Dead bug — 1–2 × 5–8/side

Progress by improving control before adding reps or sets.

Foundation+ • 3 days

Balanced full-body

Alternate sessions A and B, with at least a day between demanding sessions.

A: squat 2–3 × 8–12, push-up 2–3 × 6–12, row 2–3 × 6–12, plank 2 × 20–40 seconds.

B: Split squat 2 × 6–10/side, assisted pull-up or row 2–3 × 5–10, incline or Pike push-up 2 × 5–10, Side plank 2 × 15–30 seconds/side.

Skill-oriented • 3 sessions

Strength + technique

Use two full-body strength days and a short low-fatigue skill day.

  1. Day 1: easy skill drills, push, pull and legs
  2. Day 2: recovery or light aerobic activity
  3. Day 3: pull, push, single-leg and core strength
  4. Day 4 or 5: brief technical practice with ample rest

End skill drills before coordination deteriorates.

How to adapt: Warm up, use safe equipment, select easier variants when necessary, and allow recovery. Stop if movement causes sharp or unusual pain. Read the complete programming guide →
