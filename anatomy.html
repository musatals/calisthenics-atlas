How to read the diagrams

Mint for primary; amber for assisting.

Each exercise profile includes a deliberately simplified front-and-back human map, alongside written muscle descriptions. The colors summarize the intended learning focus; they are not measured EMG data, diagnostic markers or a claim that other muscles do not contribute.

Many exercises distribute effort across several joints, and the emphasis changes with leverage, assistance, range and individual variation. The diagrams use a stylized silhouette to preserve readability at mobile sizes.
