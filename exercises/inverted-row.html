Inverted row

With a safely rated bar, keep torso aligned while pulling chest toward the support.

Pull patternBeginner · relative difficultyDetailed technique & anatomy

What works?

Primary emphasis: Rhomboids, middle trapezius, latissimus dorsi

Important assistance: Biceps, rear shoulders, trunk

Mint = primary region · Amber = assisting region. These are simplified anatomical regions, not measurements of muscle activity. Body position changes the emphasis.

How to perform it

  1. Set a reliably anchored bar at a suitable height.
  2. Hold bar and lean back with feet supported.
  3. Pull chest toward hands while keeping pelvis and ribs aligned.
  4. Lower to straight arms without suddenly dropping the shoulders.

Breathe continuously and use a range that feels controlled and comfortable for your body.

Why this movement matters

Pulling movements involve the back and elbow flexors working together with the shoulder girdle. Grip, body angle and assistance change the relative contribution of these regions.

Progression checkpoint

Walk feet forward only once technique is stable at a higher angle.

Equipment and safety

Only use verified load-bearing equipment; accidental falls and unstable anchors are avoidable hazards.

Problem solving

Common mistakes & corrections

Using an unstable bar or non-rated furniture

Correction: Choose properly anchored exercise equipment.

Hips sag into an arch

Correction: Raise the body angle or shorten the set.
